Bridge controls

On a touchscreen the bridge is laid out for you: the engine telegraph sits at your left thumb, the wheel along the bottom, and the guns at your right. Drag anywhere on the sea to train them. The keys below work if you attach a keyboard.

Fighting the ship

Shells take time to fly. Lead the target: the aim marker shows where your salvo will land, and the range readout tells you how long it will be in the air.

Armour-piercing rewards a broadside target amidships — a citadel hit takes a cruiser apart. High explosive starts fires on anything, and is the only useful answer to armour you cannot beat.

You are only visible when spotted. Firing gives your position away; smoke and islands take it back.
