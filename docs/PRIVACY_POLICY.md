# Privacy Policy - Steel Currents

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

Steel Currents ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the Steel Currents mobile and web game ("Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### 2.1 Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information about your device when you use the Service, including:
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Game session duration
- Gameplay statistics (battles played, ships used, win/loss ratio)
- Frame rate and performance metrics
- Network connection type

### 2.2 User Accounts (if applicable)
If you create an account to play multiplayer, we collect:
- Username/Display name
- Email address (for account recovery)
- Password (encrypted)
- Friend list and social connections
- Account creation date

### 2.3 Communications
If you contact us for support, we collect:
- Your email address
- Support request content
- Attachments (error logs, screenshots)

### 2.4 Information NOT Collected
We do NOT collect:
- Real-time location data (GPS)
- Biometric data
- Financial information (game is free-to-play)
- Health data
- Any data about children under 13 without parental consent

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:
- **Service Improvement**: Analyzing gameplay patterns to fix bugs and balance gameplay
- **Performance Monitoring**: Tracking server performance and network quality
- **Player Support**: Responding to support requests and troubleshooting issues
- **Multiplayer Functionality**: Matching players, maintaining friend lists, leaderboards
- **Security**: Detecting and preventing cheating/hacking attempts
- **Analytics**: Understanding player preferences and game engagement (anonymized)

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all network communication
- Server-side data encryption for sensitive information
- Regular security audits
- Password hashing (salted bcrypt or similar)
- No storage of unnecessary personal information

**However**, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Gameplay Statistics**: Aggregated and anonymized after 1 year for analytics
- **Support Communications**: Retained for 2 years or until issue is resolved
- **Session Logs**: Automatically deleted after 90 days

## 6. Third-Party Services

### 6.1 Analytics
We may use third-party analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase) to understand game usage patterns. These services may collect:
- Device identifiers
- Gameplay metrics
- Crash reports

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

### 6.2 Cloud Hosting
Your game data is hosted on:
- AWS/DigitalOcean/similar cloud providers
- These providers comply with industry security standards
- Your data is subject to their privacy policies

### 6.3 Multiplayer Servers
Player connection data (IP address, port, bandwidth) is necessary for multiplayer functionality but is not stored permanently.

## 7. Data Sharing

We **do NOT**:
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share data for marketing purposes
- Disclose data to advertisers
- Share data without consent (except as required by law)

We **may** share data with:
- Law enforcement if required by legal process
- Our service providers who assist in game operations (under strict confidentiality agreements)
- Other players (only your username and gameplay stats in leaderboards/profiles)

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request incorrect data be corrected
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Portability**: Request your data in a standard format
- **Opt-Out**: Opt-out of non-essential data collection

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@steelcurrents.com

## 9. Children's Privacy

Steel Currents is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

For users 13-18, parents may contact us to review, delete, or restrict collection of their child's data.

## 10. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For European Union users, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation:
- Legal basis for data processing is consent and legitimate interest
- You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data
- You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
- We process data only for specified purposes
- Data is not transferred outside the EEA without your consent

## 11. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

For California residents, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act:
- You have the right to know what data we collect
- You have the right to delete your data
- You have the right to opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- You have the right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

To submit requests, contact support@steelcurrents.com

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of major changes by:
- Posting the new policy in-game
- Sending an email notification
- Requiring acceptance before continued play

Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
